**Epsilon Engine** is a data-center level inference engine built around a toolkit of efficiency knobs. The goal is to keep serving fast under changing workloads while retaining explicit quality control.

### Efficiency knobs

- Sparse attention
- KV cache compression
- KV offloading
- Verified algorithms
- Entropy compression
- MLP sparsification
- MLP skipping
- Speculative decoding
- Agentic long context
