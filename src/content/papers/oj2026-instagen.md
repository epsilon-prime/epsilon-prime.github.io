abstract: "Diffusion Transformers (DiTs) are the dominant architecture for video generation, but their quadratic attention complexity results in substantial computational cost and latency. Permuted block-sparse attention (PBSA) offers a promising approach to reducing this cost by selectively computing blocks of a semantically permuted attention matrix. We present InstaGen, which replaces top-p block selection with an exact set plus a weighted sample shared within each query cluster, improving quality at matched attention density and wall-clock time."
